# Privacy notice

UniFeedback Assistant runs only when a student opens the extension on a tab and chooses to scan or apply ratings.

- No data is sent to a developer server.
- No student credentials, student identifiers, or feedback text are collected.
- The extension reads standard dropdown labels and options on the active page to present the controls.
- The only persistent data is the last selected fill mode and the "only unanswered" preference, stored locally using browser extension storage.
- The extension does not submit, save, or finalize university feedback.

Students should review all answers and submit their own feedback honestly.
